# Travel Empire Dialer — Advisor Quick Guide

## Start the shift

1. Sign in to Travel Empire CRM with your normal CRM account.
2. Select **Dialer**. You are signed in automatically; there is no second password.
3. Choose **Ready** to join incoming calls. Use Break, Lunch, Administrative Work, or Meeting when unavailable.

## Handle a customer

1. Confirm the customer information at the top of the workspace.
2. Review the assigned campaign and the two-pane **Vacation Options / Conversation Guide** area.
3. Follow the assigned script naturally and verify the required qualification fields: citizenship, age, income, and marital/cohabitation status.
4. Complete the call, then select a disposition before moving to another contact.
5. For **Language Barrier**, choose Spanish or Unknown. For **Send Email**, choose the approved CRM template. For **Do Not Call**, confirm immediate suppression.

## Convert a call into a sale

1. Choose **Sale** only after required qualification answers are recorded.
2. Open the CRM New Sale flow and confirm the customer, package, price, and advisor.
3. Use the CRM customer-payment link when enabled; do not collect or repeat card details in the dialer.
4. Wait for the confirmed or failed payment result. On failure, schedule a callback or select the appropriate follow-up disposition.
5. Verify the booking/receipt record and customer email before ending after-call work.

## Incoming calls and messages

- Sales calls rotate among advisors who are currently in the incoming group.
- Direct extensions: Dave 1001, Nick 1002, Jake 1003; 1004 is reserved for demonstration.
- Watch the Email and Voicemail indicators. Refresh communications without refreshing the entire CRM page once the CRM endpoint is connected.

## End the shift

Choose a non-ready status, leave the incoming group, finish any after-call work, and select **Return to CRM**.
